SERVICE NOTICE

Route 3 temporary stop change

From 14 Aug, the DC Chowk Bahawalpur stop on Route 3 will shift 150m towards Model Bazar during road works near One Unit Chowk.

THIS WEEKEND

Extended hours on Route 1 & Route 2

For the Bahawalpur Cattle Fair, Route 1 and Route 2 buses will run 30 minutes later than the usual weekend schedule.

FLEET UPDATE

Two new electric buses on Route 4

Bhatta No 1 to Goth Lashkar now runs two additional EVs during peak hours, cutting the headway from 22 to 18 minutes.

PUBLIC HOLIDAY

Independence Day timetable

On 14 August all routes follow the weekend (Saturday & Sunday) schedule. Regular Monday–Friday service resumes 15 August.
